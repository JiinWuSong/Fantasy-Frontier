# Fantasy Frontier Audio Style Guide

## Global Audio Direction
Fantasy Frontier should sound adventurous, magical, elegant, and alive.
The game must not default to horror, depressive, tragic, or dark-ambient emotional tones unless a very specific scene explicitly requires it.

Core emotional pillars:
- wonder
- adventure
- warmth
- magic
- discovery
- fantasy
- scenic beauty
- hopeful momentum

## Title Screen Music
Target mood:
- grand journey begins
- horizon
- wonder
- fantasy
- elegant
- calm excitement

Must not sound like:
- horror
- depression
- dread
- mourning
- dark ambient
- emotional emptiness

Suggested palette:
- light strings
- soft woodwinds / flute
- subtle harp
- atmospheric fantasy pads
- very light choir only if tasteful
- restrained percussion

## Character Creation Music
Target mood:
- elegant
- magical
- calm
- premium
- lightly mystical
- focused but beautiful

Must not sound like:
- battle music
- horror
- sadness
- heavy drama

## Town Music
Target mood:
- welcoming
- lively
- culturally rich
- safe
- warm fantasy atmosphere

## Field / Exploration Music
Target mood:
- discovery
- movement
- wonder
- scenic adventure

## Battle Music
Target mood:
- energetic
- stylish
- adventurous
- motivating

Must not sound like:
- horror chase
- chaotic noise
- oppressive dread

## Boss Music
Target mood:
- dangerous
- memorable
- powerful
- high stakes
